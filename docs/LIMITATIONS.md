# Limits and Non-Claims

1. The project is a research simulator, not production VANET middleware.
2. PBFT is a message-level signed simulation, not a deployed distributed service.
3. The hash-linked ledger is sufficient for performance experiments but is not a public cryptocurrency blockchain.
4. Ahmed-inspired mode does not reproduce threshold ring signatures literally.
5. SUMO provides mobility, not a full physical ITS-G5 or C-V2X radio stack. Packet delivery, latency and queues are modeled by the framework after evidence creation.
6. Channel Busy Ratio is not claimed as a physical-radio measurement. A future OMNeT++/Veins coupling would be required for that claim.
7. Detector confidence is generated by deterministic plausibility rules. Final publication-quality work should validate the detector against a recognized dataset or published detector.
8. The package includes four research topologies (urban ring, corridor, intersection, and 3×3 grid). They are controlled experimental topologies, not calibrated digital twins of a real city; final claims must therefore be limited to the tested topology and load ranges.
9. No claim is made that V-PUFT is globally unique until a systematic literature review is completed.
10. Results in `results/demo_reference` are software-check results only.
