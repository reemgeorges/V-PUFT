# Phase 4: completed evidence lifecycle audit

This audit reads the 50 completed diagnostic cells. It does not rerun a cell or alter a qualification policy. Reproduce the reconciliation with `python scripts/analyze_phase4_existing_evidence.py`. The case-level file `results/post_seminar_phase4/baseline_diagnostics/fn_lifecycle_classification.csv` contains every exact `(density, seed, case_id)` and its category; `fn_exhaustive_categories_by_density_attack.csv` gives the full density and attack cross-tab. A case ID alone is not globally unique across density experiments, so always retain density and seed.

## Integrity and denominator

All 50 trace SHA-256 digests and byte lengths matched `trace_manifest_verified.csv` and their `cell_complete.json` checkpoints. All 50 checkpoint FN counts matched the case CSV counts. The Phase 4 baseline TP/FP/TN/FN matched the Phase 2 B0 metrics in every cell: **641/9/12,974/961** in total. The 961 denominator excludes terminal windows after an earlier revocation. The 50 traces are regenerated paired inputs; matching frozen counts is not proof of byte identity with the older frozen campaign.

The five failed-constraint counts are **overlapping predicates** on those same 961 FN windows: sources 943, zones 612, independent roots 96, margin 148, modalities 5. The source/zone pairs are 938 at `(1,1)`, 5 at `(0,0)`, and 18 at `(2,2)`. Exactly 18 fail margin alone under B0. Phase 1's 847 was a **mutually exclusive residual bucket**, after allocating 96 root failures and 18 margin-only failures: `961 - 96 - 18 = 847`. Every one of those 96 root failures also fails sources, so Phase 4's overlapping source count is `847 + 96 = 943`. Thus the two numbers do not conflict.

The old three-way recoverability arithmetic omitted `case-urban_roundabout-1003-veh_020-w0002` at density 100, seed 1003, attack `position_offset`. B0 fails sources, zones, and margin (`0.369951 < 0.45`). B8 finds two source and zone credits from existing distinct supporting roots, but the margin still fails. It is neither a B8 recovery nor a B0 margin-only case. The complete *counterfactual* partition is 37 B8-qualified, 905 failing a B8 physical-evidence policy requirement even with every current report, and **19** with sufficient B8 diversity but insufficient margin: `37 + 905 + 19 = 961`.

## Evidence lifecycle

The trace events identify emitted CAM/DENM messages by message/root ID, each detecting RSU, direction, and detector reason. `generated_messages.csv` provides the emitted message and sender position. The audit reconstructs the RSUs within the configured 450 m sensing radius using the scenario's 500 m SUMO net offset; this reproduces the actual detecting-RSU sets for all 961 windows. The trace and Phase 4 assignment files then connect detections to signed attestations and coordinator delivery. Across these FN windows, there are 24,408 pretransport reports and exactly 24,408 delivered reports; raw, sanitized, delivered, valid, and fresh counts agree per case. There is no observed routing loss or invalid signature in this set. The `fresh_valid_used_by_qe` label in the older diagnostic means **fresh valid input to root aggregation**, not that every report earned a final diversity credit. Root net direction, representative choice, and minimum weight still determine that credit.

At the final stage, 876 windows have exactly one RSU both in range and detecting, 84 have two, and one has three. These are **physical observation counts**, not the post-aggregation `distinct_sources` counter. In 37 windows, B0's representative selection collapses diversity even though already delivered, valid, fresh, supporting observations from different RSUs and independent roots satisfy B8's one-credit-per-root matching. The existing guarded B8 option demonstrates this algorithmic recovery counterfactually; it never makes one CAM count as two roots. No window qualifies solely by idealized extra routing: delivered and pretransport B8 results are identical.

Representative windows at density 100, seed 1001 show the distinct failure points. `veh_000-w0000` has 14 delivered reports and ten supporting roots, but only `rsu-5` was in range, so sources/zones remain 1/1. `veh_020-w0001` has 16 delivered reports from `rsu-1` and `rsu-5`, 15 supporting roots, B0 sources/zones 1/1, and observer matching 2/2; its margin is 0.966448 against 0.45. `veh_093-w0003` has 52 delivered reports from those same two RSUs but observer matching remains 1/1 and margin is -0.790192 against 0.50. `veh_020-w0002` has 30 delivered reports and B0 sources/zones 2/2, but margin 0.371523 against 0.45. Full emitted message IDs, CAM/DENM counts, detecting directions, signed attestation IDs, and root representatives are joinable by `(density, seed, case_id)` in the case-level file, assignment file, and verified trace.

## Validated mechanism and expansion decision

The prior paired B0/B1/B8 pilot on the exact `(20,1001)`, `(60,1001)`, and `(100,1001)` traces tested adversarial single-root and opposing-report guards. B8 gained 0, 1, and 2 TP over B0 respectively; its FP and FRR remained zero in these three cells. At density 100, B0 to B8 Recall was `0.392157 -> 0.440000`, P95 E2E `14,115.816 -> 14,129.509 ms`, CPU `6.046875 -> 9.515625 s`, and messages/case `17.3732 -> 17.5000`. B8 matched B1's classification in all three pilot cells and cost 3.563 s more CPU than B1 at density 100. These are measured pilot changes; **37 diagnostic qualifications are an upper bound on FN windows affected, not a measured 37-TP gain**. Phase 2's already completed 50-cell B1 run gained 28 TP over B0 (641 to 669), with FP 9 to 11, mean per-cell Recall `0.396994 -> 0.419122`, mean P95 E2E `14,106.182 -> 14,107.927 ms`, mean CPU `6.0753 -> 6.2763 s`, and mean messages/case `20.1272 -> 20.1954`. B8 has no demonstrated incremental gain over B1 and substantial pilot CPU cost. There is no justified Phase 4 full variant campaign or new QE change.

For a separate coverage study, keep six sensing RSUs and four PBFT validators (`rsu-1` through `rsu-4`) with unchanged policies, signatures, root IDs, and network model. Use topology-constrained roadside candidate sites, the 450 m sensing radius, and only seed 1001 mobility to choose and freeze six positions, maximizing windows with two genuinely distinct observing RSUs in different cells. Keep seeds 1002–1010 out of placement selection. Replay the **same saved mobility snapshots, emitted CAM/DENM inputs, attacks, and seeds** for both layouts. Re-run only sensing and downstream evidence handling for the paired layouts, verify message/root identity and four-validator membership, then pilot seeds 1002 and 1003 before any full held-out study. Report TP, FP, TN, FN, Recall, Precision, FRR, P95 E2E, CPU, and messages/case, including benign windows near added coverage. Expand only if extra supporting observations come from genuinely distinct RSUs and roots, FP/FRR remain acceptable, and the paired CPU/E2E tradeoff is justified. No such coverage pilot has been run or claimed here.

## Limits

Physical capability is modeled by the simulator's 450 m range and recorded sender positions; the files do not establish real-world radio visibility, occlusion, or sensor quality. The trace proves what this detector emitted, not what an unrecorded alternative detector would infer. The 29 multi-observer shortfalls and 19 margin failures cannot be called algorithmically recoverable from present evidence. The existing pilot is three cells and does not establish population-wide FP safety. The Phase 4 instrumentation adds attestation identity, arrival time, and evaluation time for future audits but does not itself improve Recall.
