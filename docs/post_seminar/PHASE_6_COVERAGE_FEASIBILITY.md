# Phase 6: placement feasibility and held-out checks

Training-only search used geometry and mobility seeds 1001–1006 across densities 20, 60, and 100, never attack labels, held-out seed 1007, or test Recall. It enumerated 12,653 two-site pairs over 24 road segments. The gate required coverage within one percentage point of baseline for each density and no road segment more than five points worse. 324 pairs passed. The best multi-cell pair was rsu-5=(-150,500), rsu-6=(200,-500): training multi-cell fraction .599698 versus .549370 baseline. Baseline overall coverage was .872832, .873517, .881 for densities 20,60,100. These are modeled mobility coverage results; sites are road-geometry candidates, and mounting/occlusion are unavailable.

Seed 1007 held-out pilot preserved the 87 emitted attack opportunities and detected 75 at least once in both original and candidate placements at density 100. Malicious messages observed by RSUs rose 1514 to 1528; multi-RSU, distinct-cell messages rose 481 to 607. State-aware recall changed .420 to .460 (TP 21 to 23; FP remained 0). Opportunity recall among nonterminal opportunities changed .253521 to .285714; fixed-universe protected fraction changed .390805 to .425287. At density 20, original/candidate opportunities were 19/19, observed 16/17, opportunity recall .200/.286, and original state-aware recall .364/.417. This supports a small expansion, not a full campaign.

The seed 1008 extension across all five densities is saved under `results/post_seminar_phase6_holdout/pilot_metrics.csv`. At density 100 it had FP=1 for both original and candidate, FRR .002326/.002342. Candidate TP/FN was 20/31 versus original 19/33. Placement therefore has a small, promising detection effect, with a small false-revocation cost in this cell. Spatial network paths remain fixed by RSU IDs; no geographic latency claim is made. The extension is one seed and is insufficient for significance or a deployment recommendation.

## Final held-out evidence

The frozen candidate was evaluated without retuning on seeds 1007–1010 across five densities. Coverage counts in the detection traces show no decline in attack-opportunity observation in any of the 20 pairs (three increased, 17 tied). Message-level multi-observer improvements are density dependent: the mean count across four seeds increases at densities 20 and 100 and decreases at 40–80. Fixed-denominator protected opportunity fraction improves at every density, with a +5.34 percentage-point mean paired-seed gain across densities. Full paired statistics, FP IDs, common/union analysis and resource costs are in [PHASE_6_FINAL_HELDOUT_RESULTS.md](PHASE_6_FINAL_HELDOUT_RESULTS.md). This is promising placement evidence, not proof of algorithmic improvement or deployment readiness. Spatial radio paths remain fixed in the simulator.
